## CI-complete release-candidate boundary

The v0.1 baseline is **CI-complete** after P047. That means the deterministic U/I/B/E/N/REC evidence required through P047 is green. It does **not** establish current external compatibility.

Residual live assumptions are intentionally owned by M12:

- P048 must prove MCP can attach/interact/detach against the actual PCMS-managed Persona without destroying it, and that two actual Personas are distinguishable persistent contexts.
- P048 must prove a real PROTECTED Persona uses its assigned Mullvad route and that one representative real route-loss event blocks/fails instead of falling back to Direct.
- P049 must prove the current real Perchance session/account and representative read still match the adapter contract.
- P049 must perform one disposable real Deployer mutation and independently verify the result.
- Cloudflare/provider/routing behavior may have drifted since the deterministic evidence was captured. Any discrepancy must first be encoded into the emulator/synthetic fixture and reproduced in CI before live debugging continues.

The active `FULL_V1_GAP_LEDGER.md` remains a release-scope constraint. Projects/workspaces/revision history, reusable Workflows, notifications and broader global-search/deployment-history areas remain PARTIAL or DEFERRED as recorded there. This v0.1 release candidate must not be described as **Full V1** until that later product work is completed.
